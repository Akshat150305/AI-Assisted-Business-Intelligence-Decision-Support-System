## 5. Anomalies

Weekly revenue, profit and margin were scored for 39 series over 104 complete weeks: 73 flags in 3,744 scored series-weeks (1.95%); by severity 53 moderate, 15 high, 5 extreme. 4 anomaly findings were created from recent high or extreme flags. The baseline is the median of the previous 13 weeks and a week is flagged at 4.5 or more robust deviations. A flag means 'unusual compared with recent weeks'; it does not say why and does not predict anything. Seasonal peaks can be flagged because a rolling baseline cannot anticipate them.

Against the four anomalies planted in the synthetic data, 4 of 4 were detected and 36 of the flags matched no planted event. Four events are too few to estimate accuracy; see `reports/anomaly_evaluation.md`, including how the parameters were revised.

## 8. Limitations

- Synthetic data: the patterns were designed into it. The findings show what the method reports on this data, not what a real business would see.
- Thresholds for findings and anomalies are judgement calls fixed in `app_config/config.py`; other thresholds give other findings.
- Findings are descriptive. They do not forecast and they do not establish causes.
- Two years of data allow one year-on-year comparison, which is not a trend.
- No business impact (on revenue, accuracy or efficiency) has been measured, and none is claimed.
