## Rules and thresholds

| Rule | Fires when | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| QOQ | latest complete quarter vs previous quarter, revenue or profit | change of 10% or more |
| YOY | last 12 months vs the 12 months before, revenue or profit | change of 10% or more |
| MARGIN | profit margin shift (year on year, quarter on quarter); a status finding if year on year is inside the threshold | 2 pp |
| CATMARGIN | category margin vs whole-business margin | 5 pp, share of revenue at least 2% |
| PRODUCT | product margin vs its own category's margin | 5 pp, share of revenue at least 2% |
| REGMARGIN | region margin vs whole-business margin | 3 pp |
| REGGROWTH | region year-on-year growth vs whole-business growth | 5 pp |
| ANOMALY | high or extreme anomaly flag (Phase 4) in the last 13 weeks | detector threshold |

Confidence: High if the comparison rests on at least 1,000 distinct orders in each group, Medium from 300, otherwise Low. It is lowered one level when the same quarter-on-quarter move happened a year earlier (seasonality). Anomaly findings are Medium at most.

Priority = profit at stake as a % of the current window's profit, so a revenue change, a margin gap and an anomaly share one scale.

## Limitations

- The thresholds are judgement calls fixed before the rules ran; different thresholds give different findings.
- Findings are descriptive. They do not forecast and they do not establish causes.
- With a rolling baseline, seasonal weeks can be flagged as anomalies without any problem.
- Several findings can describe the same underlying fact from different angles (for example a weak category and a weak product inside it).
