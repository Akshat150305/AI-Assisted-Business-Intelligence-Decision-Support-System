## Slide 6: 15 documented KPIs, one definition each

*Section: 5. KPI framework | status: measured*

- Revenue, profit, profit margin %, distinct orders, average order value, active customers, repeat rate and more
- Profit margin = total profit / total revenue (a ratio of totals): 36.6% in 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31
- Average order value = revenue / distinct orders: 303.39
- Change % = (current - previous) / ABS(previous); zero denominators return blank, never an error
- docs/kpi_catalogue.md lists meaning, formula, source and caveats for each

**Speaker notes:** Ratios are computed from totals, never by averaging row-level ratios.

## Slide 9: Explainable, prior-data-only anomaly detection

*Section: 7. Anomaly detection approach | status: measured*

- Weekly revenue, profit and margin for 39 series; baseline = median of the previous 13 weeks (no future data)
- Flag at 4.5 or more robust deviations (median/MAD)
- Result: 73 flags in 3,744 scored series-weeks (1.95%)
- A flag is a statistical anomaly to investigate, not a confirmed issue or a forecast
- Planted test events detected: 4 of 4 (too few to estimate accuracy)

**Speaker notes:** Disclose the parameter revision: the first settings (8 weeks, 3.5) flagged about 1 week in 20 on pure noise, so they were changed using only a noise simulation, not the planted labels.

## Slide 10: Checks suggested by the findings (not promised outcomes)

*Section: 8. Recommendations and next steps | status: measured*

- Revenue grew 16.5% year on year: Confirm which part of the change is repeatable (customers, order frequency, price) before using it in planning.
- Electronics margin is 13.9 pp below the business average: Review pricing, discounts and supplier costs for the lowest-margin products in the category before changing the range.
- Profit rose 31.4% in 2025-Q4 vs 2025-Q3: Check whether the change was expected (calendar, promotions). If it was not, review the largest contributing category and region before changing forecasts or stock plans.
- Each recommendation lists the evidence needed to confirm or reject its explanation

**Speaker notes:** Recommendations come from the rule-based engine; they are things to check or decide. Real-data validation would be the next step.
